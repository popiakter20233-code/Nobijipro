# Evening and Sleep

> *Draft status:* MEDIUM confidence. Every cited hadith carries `[NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW]` and `[HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]`. Chapter is in `drafts/`. **Sleep section (HID-0005..0007) is part of Batch 1**; sleep/wake duas section (HID-0046..0048) sits in a later batch.

---

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ approached the night the way he ﷺ approached the day: deliberately, quietly, as part of his ﷺ worship of Allah ﷻ. The Sahabah رضي الله عنهم who lived alongside him watched, and what they remembered they passed on. The narrations describe a small, specific, repeatable rhythm — preparation, posture, dhikr, recitation, and a waking that turned again to Allah ﷻ.

This chapter walks the reader through that rhythm.

---

## Preparing the body — wudu before sleep

> **Arabic:** عَنِ الْبَرَاءِ بْنِ عَازِبٍ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ: «إِذَا أَتَيْتَ مَضْجَعَكَ فَتَوَضَّأْ وُضُوءَكَ لِلصَّلَاةِ، ثُمَّ اضْطَجِعْ عَلَى شِقِّكَ الْأَيْمَنِ»
>
> **English:** *"When you go to your bed, perform wudu as you would for Salah, then lie down on your right side."*
>
> *— Narrated by Bara' ibn Azib رضي الله عنه. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 247; Sahih Muslim, no. 2710 (muttafaq alayh). See HID-0005. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL]*

The instruction is paired: a physical preparation (*wudu*) and a posture (the right side). The Prophet ﷺ taught this not as a one-off occurrence but as a settled practice for retiring at night.

---

## Preparing the bed — dusting and entrusting

> **Arabic:** عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ: «إِذَا أَوَى أَحَدُكُمْ إِلَى فِرَاشِهِ، فَلْيَنْفُضْ فِرَاشَهُ بِدَاخِلَةِ إِزَارِهِ، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَدْرِي مَا خَلَفَهُ عَلَيْهِ، ثُمَّ يَقُولُ: بِاسْمِكَ رَبِّي وَضَعْتُ جَنْبِي…»
>
> **English:** *"When one of you goes to his bed, let him dust off his bed with the inner side of his garment, for he does not know what may have come onto it. Then let him say: 'In Your name, my Lord, I lay my side…'"*
>
> *— Narrated by Abu Hurayrah رضي الله عنه. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 6320; Sahih Muslim, no. 2714 (muttafaq alayh). See HID-0048. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [TRANSLATION REVIEW]*

Two practices in one hadith — a small physical act (dusting the bed) and a dua of complete entrustment. The Prophet ﷺ does not separate care for the body from care for the soul; they are folded into the same gesture of laying down.

---

## The remembrance on retiring

> **Arabic:** عَنْ حُذَيْفَةَ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: «كَانَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ إِذَا أَخَذَ مَضْجَعَهُ مِنَ اللَّيْلِ وَضَعَ يَدَهُ تَحْتَ خَدِّهِ ثُمَّ يَقُولُ: بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا»
>
> **English:** *"When the Prophet ﷺ retired to his bed at night, he would place his hand under his cheek and say: 'In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live.'"*
>
> *— Narrated by Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman رضي الله عنه. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 6314. See HID-0006. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

The Prophet ﷺ entrusted both his ﷺ sleep — likened to a small death — and his ﷺ waking — a return to life — to Allah ﷻ. The dua is short, easy to memorize, and consistent with the framing of every breath as held by the One who created it.

---

## The recitation before sleep — the Mu'awwidhat

> **Arabic:** عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ كَانَ إِذَا أَوَى إِلَى فِرَاشِهِ كُلَّ لَيْلَةٍ جَمَعَ كَفَّيْهِ ثُمَّ نَفَثَ فِيهِمَا فَقَرَأَ فِيهِمَا: ﴿قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ﴾ وَ﴿قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ﴾ وَ﴿قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ﴾ ثُمَّ يَمْسَحُ بِهِمَا مَا اسْتَطَاعَ مِنْ جَسَدِهِ
>
> **English:** *"When the Prophet ﷺ went to bed every night, he would cup his hands together, blow into them, and recite Surah al-Ikhlas, Surah al-Falaq, and Surah an-Nas in them, then wipe his hands over as much of his body as he could reach."*
>
> *— Narrated by Aishah رضي الله عنها. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 5017. See HID-0007. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

Aishah رضي الله عنها describes a deliberate nightly practice. The choice of three Surahs is significant: each one names Allah ﷻ as Lord and refuge. The Prophet ﷺ wrapped himself in their meanings before laying down.

A second nightly recitation, paired with these three, was Ayat al-Kursi:

> **Arabic:** عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رضي الله عنه فِي قِصَّةِ الشَّيْطَانِ قَالَ: «إِذَا أَوَيْتَ إِلَى فِرَاشِكَ فَاقْرَأْ آيَةَ الْكُرْسِيِّ مِنْ أَوَّلِهَا حَتَّى تَخْتِمَ الْآيَةَ، فَإِنَّكَ لَنْ يَزَالَ عَلَيْكَ مِنَ اللَّهِ حَافِظٌ، وَلَا يَقْرَبَنَّكَ شَيْطَانٌ حَتَّى تُصْبِحَ»
>
> **English:** *"(In the narration where the devil told Abu Hurayrah:) 'When you go to your bed, recite Ayat al-Kursi from its beginning to its end. A protector from Allah will remain with you, and no devil will come near you until morning.'"*
>
> *— Narrated by Abu Hurayrah رضي الله عنه. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 5010. See HID-0047. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [TRANSLATION REVIEW]*

The fuller narration is striking — Abu Hurayrah رضي الله عنه caught a thief who turned out to be a devil; the Prophet ﷺ confirmed that the advice the devil gave was true. The chapter retains the relevant clause; the full story belongs to a longer treatment.

## The waking — gratitude returning

> **Arabic:** عَنْ حُذَيْفَةَ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: كَانَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ إِذَا اسْتَيْقَظَ مِنَ اللَّيْلِ قَالَ: «الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا، وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ»
>
> **English:** *"When the Prophet ﷺ awoke at night, he ﷺ would say: 'Praise be to Allah Who has given us life after He caused us to die, and to Him is the resurrection.'"*
>
> *— Narrated by Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman رضي الله عنه. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 6312. See HID-0046. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

The waking dua mirrors the sleeping dua exactly: *Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya* before sleep → *Alhamdulillah ladhi ahyana* on waking. Each act is named in relation to the other. The night, in this framework, is not a gap in worship but a small pilgrimage from one praise to the next.

---

## Practical lessons

- The Prophet ﷺ performed *wudu* before sleep (HID-0005).
- He ﷺ lay on his right side with his hand under his cheek (HID-0005, HID-0006).
- He ﷺ dusted his bed and committed his soul to Allah ﷻ on lying down (HID-0048).
- He ﷺ said "*Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya*" (HID-0006).
- He ﷺ recited *Surah al-Ikhlas*, *Surah al-Falaq*, and *Surah an-Nas*, blew into his hands, and wiped them over his body (HID-0007).
- He ﷺ taught reciting *Ayat al-Kursi* before sleep for protection through the night (HID-0047).
- On waking, he ﷺ said "*Alhamdulillah ladhi ahyana*…" (HID-0046).

The night, in this chapter, is not a gap in worship — it is itself a small *journey*. The body prepares; the bed is dusted; the dhikr is said; the surahs are recited; the soul is entrusted. And in the morning the praise that closed one journey opens the next.
