# Speech and Manners

> *Draft status:* MEDIUM. HID-0015 carries `[HISTORICAL REVIEW]` (Tier-3 primary). Not yet promoted to `verified/`.

---

The Sahabah رضي الله عنهم who walked beside the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ noticed everything. The way he ﷺ moved. The way he ﷺ spoke. The way he ﷺ smiled — for he ﷺ smiled often. They preserved these small features not as mere description but as evidence of a complete man, a complete Prophet ﷺ. This chapter walks through three: how he ﷺ moved, how he ﷺ spoke, how he ﷺ smiled.

---

# Part 1 — How he ﷺ walked

## The gait — descending from a height

> **Arabic:** عَنْ عَلِيٍّ رضي الله عنه فِي صِفَةِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: «إِذَا مَشَى تَقَلَّعَ كَأَنَّمَا يَنْحَطُّ مِنْ صَبَبٍ»
>
> **English:** *"When he ﷺ walked, he stepped briskly, as if he were descending from a height."*
>
> *— Ali ibn Abi Talib رضي الله عنه. Shamail al-Muhammadiyyah, no. 1; Jami` at-Tirmidhi, no. 3637. See HID-0015. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL]*

This is part of the *hilyah* — the description of the Prophet's ﷺ appearance and bearing. Ali رضي الله عنه describes a deliberate, vigorous gait, not a casual stroll.

## The pace — as if the earth folded for him

> **Arabic:** عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: «مَا رَأَيْتُ شَيْئًا أَحْسَنَ مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ، كَأَنَّ الشَّمْسَ تَجْرِي فِي وَجْهِهِ، وَمَا رَأَيْتُ أَحَدًا أَسْرَعَ فِي مِشْيَتِهِ مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ، كَأَنَّمَا الْأَرْضُ تُطْوَى لَهُ»
>
> **English:** *"I never saw anything more beautiful than the Messenger of Allah ﷺ — as though the sun were running across his face. And I never saw anyone walk faster than the Messenger of Allah ﷺ — as though the earth were folded for him."*
>
> *— Abu Hurayrah رضي الله عنه. Jami` at-Tirmidhi, no. 3648. See HID-0016. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

Abu Hurayrah رضي الله عنه, who walked with the Prophet ﷺ daily, gives a striking pair of similes — light on his ﷺ face, the earth folding for him ﷺ. Both images communicate energy and presence.

# Part 2 — How he ﷺ spoke

## Speech — clear and distinct

> **Arabic:** عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها قَالَتْ: «مَا كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ يَسْرُدُ الْحَدِيثَ سَرْدَكُمْ هَذَا، وَلَكِنَّهُ كَانَ يَتَكَلَّمُ بِكَلَامٍ بَيِّنٍ فَصْلٍ، يَحْفَظُهُ مَنْ جَلَسَ إِلَيْهِ»
>
> **English:** *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ would not rattle off speech the way you do. Rather, his speech was clear and distinct — anyone sitting with him would be able to remember it."*
>
> *— Aishah رضي الله عنها. Sunan Abu Dawud, no. 4839. See HID-0017. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

Aishah رضي الله عنها is correcting a person's pace in narrating. She compares it to the Prophet's ﷺ manner: his ﷺ speech was paced for *retention* — anyone listening could absorb and memorize.

## The choice — speak good or be silent

> **Arabic:** عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رضي الله عنه عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: «مَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ فَلْيَقُلْ خَيْرًا أَوْ لِيَصْمُتْ»
>
> **English:** *"Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, let him speak good or remain silent."*
>
> *— Abu Hurayrah رضي الله عنه. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 6018; Sahih Muslim, no. 47 (muttafaq alayh). See HID-0018. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

The Prophet ﷺ ties speech to *iman*. The choice he ﷺ presents is binary — beneficial speech, or silence. There is no third permitted category.

# Part 3 — How he ﷺ smiled

## The smile, not full laughter

> **Arabic:** عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها قَالَتْ: «مَا رَأَيْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ مُسْتَجْمِعًا قَطُّ ضَاحِكًا حَتَّى تُرَى مِنْهُ لَهَوَاتُهُ، إِنَّمَا كَانَ يَتَبَسَّمُ»
>
> **English:** *"I never saw the Messenger of Allah ﷺ laughing fully so that his uvula was visible. He would only smile."*
>
> *— Aishah رضي الله عنها. Jami` at-Tirmidhi, no. 3641. See HID-0043. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

Aishah رضي الله عنها describes a measured demeanour — neither cold nor unrestrained. The smile was the form his ﷺ joy took.

## The smile that greeted everyone

> **Arabic:** عَنْ جَرِيرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: «مَا حَجَبَنِي النَّبِيُّ ﷺ مُنْذُ أَسْلَمْتُ، وَلَا رَآنِي إِلَّا تَبَسَّمَ فِي وَجْهِي»
>
> **English:** *"The Prophet ﷺ never barred me from his presence since I embraced Islam, and he ﷺ never saw me without smiling in my face."*
>
> *— Jarir ibn Abdullah al-Bajali رضي الله عنه. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 6089; Sahih Muslim, no. 2475 (muttafaq alayh). See HID-0044. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL]*

Jarir رضي الله عنه describes a *consistent* practice. Every meeting opened with the Prophet's ﷺ smile.

## The smile as charity

> **Arabic:** عَنْ أَبِي ذَرٍّ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: «تَبَسُّمُكَ فِي وَجْهِ أَخِيكَ لَكَ صَدَقَةٌ»
>
> **English:** *"Your smile in your brother's face is a charity for you."*
>
> *— Abu Dharr al-Ghifari رضي الله عنه. Jami` at-Tirmidhi, no. 1956. See HID-0045. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL]*

The Prophet ﷺ names the smile as a *ṣadaqah* — a charitable act. It costs nothing and is counted in the giver's favour.

## Practical lessons
- The Prophet ﷺ walked with vigor, with presence (HID-0015, HID-0016).
- His ﷺ speech was paced for retention — anyone could absorb it (HID-0017).
- He ﷺ tied beneficial speech to faith itself (HID-0018).
- His ﷺ laughter was a smile, not full-throated laughter (HID-0043).
- He ﷺ greeted Sahabah with a smile every time (HID-0044).
- He ﷺ named a smile in a brother's face as sadaqah (HID-0045).
