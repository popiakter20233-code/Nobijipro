# Food and Drink

> *Draft status:* MEDIUM. HID-0014 carries `[FIQH REVIEW]` (a prohibition). Not yet promoted to `verified/`.

---

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ ate simply, and the Sahabah رضي الله عنهم who shared his meals preserved a small set of recurring details: he ﷺ began with the name of Allah ﷻ, ate with his ﷺ right hand, ate from what was nearest, paused while drinking, and praised Allah ﷻ when finished. Each detail is small in itself; together they describe a manner. This chapter walks through them in the order of a meal — beginning, drinking, ending.

---

## Beginning the meal

> **Arabic:** عَنْ عُمَرَ بْنِ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: قَالَ لِي رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ: «يَا غُلَامُ، سَمِّ اللَّهَ، وَكُلْ بِيَمِينِكَ، وَكُلْ مِمَّا يَلِيكَ»
>
> **English:** *"O young boy, mention the name of Allah, eat with your right hand, and eat from what is near you."*
>
> *— Umar ibn Abi Salamah رضي الله عنه. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 5376; Sahih Muslim, no. 2022 (muttafaq alayh). See HID-0011. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL]*

The Prophet ﷺ taught these three things to a young Sahabi at a meal — concise, memorable, and easy to follow: name Allah ﷻ, right hand, what's nearest. The instructions describe the *opening* of every meal.

---

## Drinking in three breaths

> **Arabic:** عَنْ أَنَسٍ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: «كَانَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ يَتَنَفَّسُ فِي الشَّرَابِ ثَلَاثًا»
>
> **English:** *"The Prophet ﷺ would take three breaths while drinking (taking the cup away from his mouth between sips)."*
>
> *— Anas ibn Malik رضي الله عنه. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 5631; Sahih Muslim, no. 2028 (muttafaq alayh). See HID-0013. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL]*

Anas رضي الله عنه describes a habitual rhythm — three pauses, breath taken outside the cup. The narration preserves a small physical detail of the Prophet's ﷺ manner.

## And not breathing into the vessel

> **Arabic:** عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رضي الله عنهما أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ نَهَى أَنْ يُتَنَفَّسَ فِي الْإِنَاءِ، أَوْ يُنْفَخَ فِيهِ
>
> **English:** *"The Prophet ﷺ forbade breathing into the drinking vessel, or blowing into it."*
>
> *— Ibn Abbas رضي الله عنهما. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 5630; Sahih Muslim, no. 267. See HID-0014. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL] [FIQH REVIEW]*

The Prophet's ﷺ pause-and-breathe-outside practice in the prior hadith is paired here with an explicit prohibition: do not breathe *into* the cup. The two narrations together form a complete picture — what to do, and what not to do.

---

## The praise after the meal

> **Arabic:** عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ: «إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَيَرْضَى عَنِ الْعَبْدِ أَنْ يَأْكُلَ الْأَكْلَةَ فَيَحْمَدَهُ عَلَيْهَا، أَوْ يَشْرَبَ الشَّرْبَةَ فَيَحْمَدَهُ عَلَيْهَا»
>
> **English:** *"Indeed, Allah is pleased with His servant who praises Him when he eats a meal or drinks a drink."*
>
> *— Anas ibn Malik رضي الله عنه. Sahih Muslim, no. 2734. See HID-0012. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL]*

The Prophet ﷺ frames eating and drinking as occasions for *hamd* — praise. The narration describes a state of divine pleasure tied to a small daily act. Even something as ordinary as a meal becomes, in this framing, a moment Allah ﷻ takes note of when it ends in His praise.

---

## Practical lessons

- The Prophet ﷺ began the meal with the name of Allah ﷻ (HID-0011).
- He ﷺ ate with his right hand (HID-0011).
- He ﷺ ate from what was nearest to him (HID-0011).
- He ﷺ took three breaths while drinking, away from the cup (HID-0013).
- He ﷺ forbade breathing or blowing into the vessel (HID-0014).
- He ﷺ described divine pleasure with the one who praises Allah ﷻ for food and drink (HID-0012).
