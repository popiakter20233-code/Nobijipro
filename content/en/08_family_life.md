# Family Life

> *Draft status:* MEDIUM. Not yet promoted to `verified/`.

---

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ's home was small, simple, and watched. His ﷺ wives, children, grandchildren, and household servants lived alongside him, and what they saw they preserved. The narrations describe a man who began the home at the threshold — with a dua, with salam — and who, once inside, served those whom others would expect to be served. This chapter walks from the door inward.

---

# Part 1 — At the Threshold

## Leaving home — entrusting the day

> **Arabic:** عَنْ أَنَسٍ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: «إِذَا خَرَجَ الرَّجُلُ مِنْ بَيْتِهِ فَقَالَ: بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ، يُقَالُ لَهُ: هُدِيتَ، وَكُفِيتَ، وَوُقِيتَ»
>
> **English:** *"When a person leaves his house and says: 'In the name of Allah, I rely upon Allah; there is no power and no strength except with Allah,' it is said to him: 'You are guided, sufficed, and protected.'"*
>
> *— Anas ibn Malik رضي الله عنه. Sunan Abu Dawud, no. 5096. See HID-0028. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL]*

The Prophet ﷺ teaches a short, three-clause dua for the threshold. Each clause names a different relationship — opening, attitude, confession of weakness. The reply — guidance, sufficiency, protection — covers the three risks of stepping outside.

## Entering with dhikr

> **Arabic:** عَنْ جَابِرٍ رضي الله عنه عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: «إِذَا دَخَلَ الرَّجُلُ بَيْتَهُ، فَذَكَرَ اللَّهَ عِنْدَ دُخُولِهِ وَعِنْدَ طَعَامِهِ، قَالَ الشَّيْطَانُ: لَا مَبِيتَ لَكُمْ وَلَا عَشَاءَ»
>
> **English:** *"When a man enters his house and remembers Allah at his entry and at his meal, Satan says: 'There is no place to stay for you and no dinner.'"*
>
> *— Jabir ibn Abdullah رضي الله عنه. Sahih Muslim, no. 2018. See HID-0029. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL]*

The Prophet ﷺ ties two thresholds together — entry and meal — as paired moments where dhikr functions as protection.

## A note on the foot — practical inference

The widespread teaching that **the right foot enters the home and the left foot leaves** is **a practical application of the broader Sunnah principle** that the Prophet ﷺ loved beginning from the right in noble matters (HID-0052). It is consistent with the principle and with the sandals hadith (HID-0053). It is not, however, in a directly-stated hadith with Sahihayn-grade specific authentication. Practical inference, clearly labeled.

## Salam on entering one's family

> **Arabic:** عَنْ أَنَسٍ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: قَالَ لِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: «يَا بُنَيَّ، إِذَا دَخَلْتَ عَلَى أَهْلِكَ، فَسَلِّمْ، يَكُونُ بَرَكَةً عَلَيْكَ وَعَلَى أَهْلِ بَيْتِكَ»
>
> **English:** *"O my dear son, when you enter upon your family, give salam — it will be a blessing upon you and upon the people of your household."*
>
> *— Anas ibn Malik رضي الله عنه. Jami` at-Tirmidhi, no. 2698. See HID-0030. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

The Prophet ﷺ describes salam not just as etiquette but as *barakah* — a household-level blessing.

# Part 2 — Inside the Household

## The standard — best to one's family

> **Arabic:** عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها قَالَتْ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: «خَيْرُكُمْ خَيْرُكُمْ لِأَهْلِهِ، وَأَنَا خَيْرُكُمْ لِأَهْلِي»
>
> **English:** *"The best of you is the best to his family, and I am the best of you to my family."*
>
> *— Aishah رضي الله عنها. Jami` at-Tirmidhi, no. 3895. See HID-0031. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

The Prophet ﷺ sets a standard and places himself ﷺ within it. The clause structure repeats the word *khayrukum* — *the best of you* — three times in close sequence; the repetition is part of the meaning.

## The practice — service in the household

> **Arabic:** عَنِ الْأَسْوَدِ قَالَ: سَأَلْتُ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها: مَا كَانَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ يَصْنَعُ فِي بَيْتِهِ؟ قَالَتْ: «كَانَ يَكُونُ فِي مِهْنَةِ أَهْلِهِ — تَعْنِي خِدْمَةَ أَهْلِهِ — فَإِذَا حَضَرَتِ الصَّلَاةُ خَرَجَ إِلَى الصَّلَاةِ»
>
> **English:** *"Al-Aswad asked Aishah رضي الله عنها: 'What did the Prophet ﷺ do in his house?' She said: 'He used to be in the service of his family — meaning, helping with their housework — and when the time of Salah came, he ﷺ would go out to pray.'"*
>
> *— Aishah رضي الله عنها (via al-Aswad). Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 676. See HID-0032. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

Aishah رضي الله عنها answers a direct question with a direct answer. The phrase *fī mihnati ahlihi* — literally, "in his family's task" — is striking; it inverts the assumption that the head of the household waits to be served.

## Mercy with children

> **Arabic:** عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رضي الله عنه أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ قَبَّلَ الْحَسَنَ بْنَ عَلِيٍّ، وَعِنْدَهُ الْأَقْرَعُ بْنُ حَابِسٍ التَّمِيمِيُّ جَالِسًا، فَقَالَ الْأَقْرَعُ: إِنَّ لِي عَشَرَةً مِنَ الْوَلَدِ مَا قَبَّلْتُ مِنْهُمْ أَحَدًا. فَنَظَرَ إِلَيْهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ ثُمَّ قَالَ: «مَنْ لَا يَرْحَمُ لَا يُرْحَمُ»
>
> **English:** *"The Prophet ﷺ kissed al-Hasan ibn Ali, and al-Aqra' ibn Habis at-Tamimi was sitting with him. Al-Aqra' said: 'I have ten children and I have never kissed any of them.' The Messenger of Allah ﷺ looked at him and said: 'Whoever does not show mercy will not be shown mercy.'"*
>
> *— Abu Hurayrah رضي الله عنه. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 5997. See HID-0033. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL — al-Hasan ibn Ali]*

The narration moves through three beats — the affection (the kiss), the contrast (the bedouin's pride), and the principle (the Prophet's ﷺ short, complete reply). The mercy a man shows or fails to show his children is, in this hadith, the mercy he himself will receive.

## Practical lessons
- The Prophet ﷺ taught a specific dua for leaving home (HID-0028).
- He ﷺ taught that dhikr at entry and meal protects against Satan's company (HID-0029).
- He ﷺ instructed giving salam on entering one's family as a *barakah* (HID-0030).
- He ﷺ named being best to one's family as the standard of being best (HID-0031).
- He ﷺ helped with the household work (HID-0032).
- He ﷺ kissed his ﷺ grandson openly and connected mercy received with mercy given (HID-0033).
