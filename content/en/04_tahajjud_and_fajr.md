# Tahajjud and Fajr

> *Draft status:* MEDIUM. Carries `[GRADE DISPUTED]` on HID-0023 and `[FIQH REVIEW]` on HID-0023 and HID-0025. Not yet promoted to `verified/`.

---

The night was where the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ went to meet Allah ﷻ alone. The Sahabah رضي الله عنهم who saw him pray *Tahajjud* — and the morning adhkar he ﷺ recited as the day began — preserved a quiet, settled rhythm. This chapter walks through both: the long standing in the dark, and the remembrance that opens the day.

---

# Part 1 — Tahajjud

## The standing rule — eleven raka'at

> **Arabic:** عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها قَالَتْ: «مَا كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ يَزِيدُ فِي رَمَضَانَ وَلَا فِي غَيْرِهِ عَلَى إِحْدَى عَشْرَةَ رَكْعَةً»
>
> **English:** *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ would not pray more than eleven raka'at, neither in Ramadan nor outside it."*
>
> *— Aishah رضي الله عنها. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 1147; Sahih Muslim, no. 738 (muttafaq alayh). See HID-0025. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [FIQH REVIEW]*

Aishah رضي الله عنها describes a stable practice — the same in Ramadan and outside it. The chapter retains this as description; jurisprudential debates on minimum and maximum counts are deferred to scholar discussion.

## The length of his ﷺ standing — until his ﷺ feet swelled

> **Arabic:** عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ كَانَ يَقُومُ مِنَ اللَّيْلِ حَتَّى تَتَفَطَّرَ قَدَمَاهُ، فَقَالَتْ: لِمَ تَصْنَعُ هَذَا يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ وَقَدْ غَفَرَ اللَّهُ لَكَ مَا تَقَدَّمَ مِنْ ذَنْبِكَ وَمَا تَأَخَّرَ؟ قَالَ: «أَفَلَا أَكُونُ عَبْدًا شَكُورًا»
>
> **English:** *"The Prophet ﷺ used to stand at night until his feet would swell. Aishah رضي الله عنها said: 'Why do you do this, O Messenger of Allah, when Allah has forgiven you what came before of your sins and what is to come?' He ﷺ said: 'Should I not be a grateful servant?'"*
>
> *— Aishah رضي الله عنها. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 4837; Sahih Muslim, no. 2820 (muttafaq alayh). See HID-0026. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

The Prophet's ﷺ answer reframes Tahajjud entirely. He ﷺ does not stand to *attain* what is already given; he ﷺ stands because gratitude wants standing.

## The recitation — long, deliberate, multi-surah

> **Arabic:** عَنْ حُذَيْفَةَ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: صَلَّيْتُ مَعَ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ ذَاتَ لَيْلَةٍ فَافْتَتَحَ الْبَقَرَةَ… ثُمَّ افْتَتَحَ النِّسَاءَ فَقَرَأَهَا، ثُمَّ افْتَتَحَ آلَ عِمْرَانَ فَقَرَأَهَا، يَقْرَأُ مُتَرَسِّلًا
>
> **English:** *"I prayed one night with the Prophet ﷺ. He began Surah al-Baqarah… then he opened Surah an-Nisa' and recited it, then he opened Aal Imran and recited it — reciting unhurriedly."*
>
> *— Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman رضي الله عنه. Sahih Muslim, no. 772. See HID-0027. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED]*

Hudhayfah رضي الله عنه describes a single rakah of remarkable length — three of the longest surahs in sequence, recited *mutarassilan* (unhurriedly).

# Part 2 — Morning Adhkar After Fajr

After the long night came a different posture: a quiet sitting, a turn toward Allah ﷻ in the first hour of light.

## Sayyid al-Istighfar

> **Arabic:** عَنْ شَدَّادِ بْنِ أَوْسٍ رضي الله عنه عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: «سَيِّدُ الِاسْتِغْفَارِ أَنْ تَقُولَ: اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ…»
>
> **English:** *"The master of seeking forgiveness is to say: 'O Allah, You are my Lord, there is no god but You. You created me and I am Your servant…'"*  *(full text in source)*
>
> *— Shaddad ibn Aws رضي الله عنه. Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 6306. See HID-0022. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL] [TRANSLATION REVIEW]*

The Prophet ﷺ specified a precise wording and named it the *master* of istighfar. The full text is longer than the excerpt; readers should consult a reliable published translator.

## Sitting in dhikr until sunrise

> **Arabic:** عَنْ أَنَسٍ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: «مَنْ صَلَّى الْغَدَاةَ فِي جَمَاعَةٍ ثُمَّ قَعَدَ يَذْكُرُ اللَّهَ حَتَّى تَطْلُعَ الشَّمْسُ ثُمَّ صَلَّى رَكْعَتَيْنِ كَانَتْ لَهُ كَأَجْرِ حَجَّةٍ وَعُمْرَةٍ، تَامَّةٍ تَامَّةٍ تَامَّةٍ»
>
> **English:** *"Whoever prays Fajr in congregation, then sits remembering Allah until the sun rises, then prays two raka'at, will have a reward like that of a complete Hajj and Umrah — complete, complete, complete."*
>
> *— Anas ibn Malik رضي الله عنه. Jami` at-Tirmidhi, no. 586. See HID-0023. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [GRADE DISPUTED] [FIQH REVIEW]*

The narration describes a specific reward attached to a specific sequence (Fajr in congregation → sustained dhikr until sunrise → two raka'at). The grading is debated among graders — Tirmidhi rates it *hasan*, while some have classed it weak. The chapter presents it explicitly for scholar adjudication.

## Asbahna wa asbah al-mulku lillah

> **Arabic:** عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: كَانَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ إِذَا أَمْسَى قَالَ: «أَمْسَيْنَا وَأَمْسَى الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ…» وَإِذَا أَصْبَحَ قَالَ ذَلِكَ أَيْضًا
>
> **English:** *"When evening came, the Prophet ﷺ would say: 'We have entered evening and the dominion belongs to Allah; praise be to Allah; there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner…'; and when morning came he ﷺ would say it likewise."*
>
> *— Abdullah ibn Mas'ud رضي الله عنه. Sahih Muslim, no. 2723. See HID-0024. [NEEDS SCHOLAR REVIEW] [HUMAN REVIEW RECOMMENDED] [NAME PROVISIONAL]*

Ibn Mas'ud رضي الله عنه describes a paired adhkar — the same opening framework recited morning and evening with the verb adjusted (*asbahna* / *amsayna*). The day and the evening, in this practice, are bracketed by the same affirmation.

## Practical lessons
- The Prophet ﷺ regularly prayed eleven raka'at in qiyam al-layl (HID-0025).
- He ﷺ stood long enough that his ﷺ feet would swell (HID-0026).
- He ﷺ understood his standing as gratitude (HID-0026).
- He ﷺ recited long passages slowly and deliberately (HID-0027).
- He ﷺ named *Sayyid al-Istighfar* — a specific master-form of seeking forgiveness (HID-0022).
- He ﷺ described a Hajj-and-Umrah-equivalent reward for sustained post-Fajr dhikr (HID-0023; grade disputed).
- He ﷺ recited paired *asbahna*/*amsayna* adhkar morning and evening (HID-0024).
